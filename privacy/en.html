Pofu Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what data the Pofu mobile game (“the app”), developed by Oguzhan Atlan, processes and how.

1. Data we collect

The app needs no account and never asks for personal information such as your name, email or location. Your scores, settings, album progress and daily puzzle results are stored only on your device; they are not sent to us or to any server. Deleting the app deletes them too.

2. No ads or third-party services

The app shows no ads. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services and shares no data with third parties. If ads are ever added, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

3. Permissions

  • Reminders: if you allow it, the app shows at most one reminder notification a day for the Daily Puzzle. Notifications are scheduled on your device and no data is sent for them. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings or in your device settings.

4. Children

The app is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Your rights (GDPR and KVKK)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation and Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) you have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your data. Since we keep no data outside your device, you can remove the app’s data by deleting the app. For any questions, contact us.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its effective date.
